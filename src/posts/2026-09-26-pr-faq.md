My attempt to articulate the purpose of this blog, using a framework popularized by Amazon's Colin Bryar and Bill Carr in the book [Working Backwards](https://www.amazon.com/Working-Backwards-Insights-Stories-Secrets/dp/1250267595/ref=tmm_hrd_swatch_0). The idea is to write a one- to two-page press release for the product you are building, followed by an FAQ to answer any important implementation questions.
<br><br>
My hope with this framework is that you, the reader, understand if this is going to be a valuable resource to you. There are so many blogs and articles out there vying for your attention, and life is so short, so please remember Mary Oliver, who aptly noted in her novel, *Upstream*:

> "Attention is the beginning of devotion."

# Insightful, Timely Reflections from a Human

Sept 26, 2026 - While AI and large players threaten new writers from breaking into the online community, one somehow found the backdoor. Ethan's blog is a heartfelt, honest, entertaining, and intriguing place where you'll walk away with new ways to think about the world. Primarily writing about technology, business, culture, and film, the blog has a beautifully human touch that sets it apart from the otherwise sanitized writing that's permeating the online landscape.
<br><br>
Despite the natural optimism, his writing displays groundedness in his hopeful ideas. Politics aren't raised often, but if they are, they're done with fairness applied to all sides. "When a new article drops, it's the next thing I read in my queue," commented a reader, "the articles are short, but offer unique and powerful ideas and questions to consider about the things I often take for granted."
<br><br>
What's more, the articles are only available on Ethan's GitHub-supported website. A fairly low-tech setup that ironically supports such worldspanning, inspiring, and thought-provoking ideas.

## Frequently-asked Questions

### Will you expand to other platforms (e.g., Substack, Medium, a better website)?
Possibly, though right now, this is a sufficient platform to share my thoughts with the world in a way that I can manage.

### Do you use AI when writing your content?
I do, but only to check my grammar and the facts that I cite. The ideas and sentences you read are human-generated, for better or for worse.

### Who is your target audience?
Anybody who is interested in challenging their thinking, learning, and can appreciate many things.

### How often will you publish?
I'll start with once a month. I'd be up for once a week but want to ensure I can maintain content quality if I do.

### How will you handle corrections, criticisms, conflicts of interest, and sensitive topics?
The fear of criticism is real, and I'm not sure it it'll ever go away completely for me. This was largely the reason I decided to start this series--to get outside of my head and share what I see, think, and feel with others in the hopes that something in this resonates with them. If something accomplishes the opposite effect, I'd love to know why. Please send me an email to let me know at _________.
<br><br>
If what I share is too much, there is nothing keeping you here and you are free to explore other writings from other people.

### Premortem: how would you expect this blog to fail?
I lose the courage to keep writing. I was never the best writer in school and the insecurity still stays with me to this day. Despite this, I still write every day, whether in public or to myself. Maybe in the act of publishing my writings I'll discover that I am more capable than I thought.
<br><br>
The other obvious risk is time. I have a lot on my plate with family, friends, work, and other hobbies. I'll continue writing to the best of my abilities, but if this means of writing is no longer suiting my goals and aspirations, I'll stop.
